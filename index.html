Introduction

A message to you fellow comrades

I stand before you not just as a candidate, but as one of you. We share the same journey far from home, building our future.

This manifesto represents a commitment to unity, welfare, and opportunity for every Ugandan student in Algeria.

Vision

Where we are going together

  • 🌍 Unity

    Build a strong and connected Ugandan student community.

  • 💙 Support

    Ensure every student has access to guidance and assistance.

  • 🚀 Growth

    Create opportunities for academic and personal development.

Community

Building connection and belonging

  • 👥 Big Sibling Program

    Pair new students with experienced ones for guidance.

  • 🎉 Cultural Events

    Celebrate identity and strengthen unity.

  • 🤝 Inclusion

    Ensure every student feels welcomed and valued.

  • ⚽ Social Activities

    Organize sports and networking events.

Welfare

Supporting student well-being

  • 💊 Health Support

    Assist students in accessing healthcare services.

  • 💰 Emergency Assistance

    Create a system to support students in urgent situations.

  • 📄 Documentation Help

    Guide students through residence and legal processes.

  • 💙 Mental Health

    Promote awareness and peer support systems.

Academics

Empowering academic success

  • 📚 Study Groups

    Encourage collaboration among students.

  • 🎓 Mentorship

    Connect juniors with experienced students.

  • 🧠 Workshops

    Provide academic and skill-building sessions.

  • 🌐 Opportunities

    Share internships and career opportunities.

Leadership

Transparent and accountable leadership

  • 📢 Open Communication

    Keep students informed and involved.

  • 🧾 Transparency

    Ensure accountability in all activities.

  • ⚖ Fair Representation

    Represent all students equally.

  • 🤲 Service

    Leadership as a responsibility, not privilege.

Closing Message

A call to action

Together, we can build a stronger, united, and thriving student community.

Your vote is not just support, it is a step toward a better future for all of us.

Vote for Leadership. Vote for Unity. Vote for Progress.
